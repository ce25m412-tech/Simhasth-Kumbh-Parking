Smart Loading Zones — design methodology

3-step workflow: greenfield clustering → optimal service assignment → hourly stall sizing.

Step 1 — Greenfield clustering

Each establishment j is assigned a demand weight τj. Weighted k-means++ or DBSCAN partitions establishments into N candidate service clusters, minimising the weighted sum of squared walking distances to the cluster centroid.

τ_j    = λ · μ / θ                   demand weight of establishment j
WSW_j  = τ_j · ‖x_j − n_i‖²          weighted squared distance to centroid n_i
φ      = Σ_x min_n WSW               objective minimised by the clustering

N is swept from 1 up to N_max;
coverage is measured as the fraction of weight whose walk ≤ d_max.

k-means++ gives compact weighted clusters; DBSCAN (ε, minPts) tolerates arbitrary shapes and labels outlier establishments. "Auto" picks whichever achieves higher coverage.

Step 2 — Optimal service assignment

Each establishment is assigned to at most one SLZ candidate site. Walking distance is bounded by dmax; each site has a reserved kerb capacity Ki expressed in stalls.

min  Σ_i Σ_j d_ij · e_ij
s.t. Σ_i e_ij = 1              ∀ j
     d_ij ≤ d_max              ∀ i, j with e_ij = 1
     Σ_j e_ij · τ_jt ≤ S_it ≤ K_i
     e_ij ∈ {0,1}

Kerb is allocated exclusively: the same metre cannot serve two zones. Any overlap or junction-setback block is removed from the candidate kerb before assignment.

Step 3 — Hourly stall sizing

Demand is spread over the operating hours by an arrival profile (flat, calibrated shape, or custom). For each site and each hour t the required stalls are:

S_it = ceil( a_it · LS )     a_it = offered load (hours of service)
LS ∈ {1, 2, 4}               coincident / peak / average
S_it = ⌈ a_it / θ ⌉          alternative occupancy-based rule

LS follows the level-of-service calibration: 4 for an average-demand assumption, 2 for peak, 1 for coincident (worst case). The site's peak-hour Sit is the number of stalls visualised on the map.

Placement rule (implemented here)

SLZ sites are placed only on kerb adjacent to parking (outer / inner) and holding areas. They are never placed inside those polygons, and entry / exit driveways of those areas are removed from the candidate kerb set.
